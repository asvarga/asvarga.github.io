How rules work

A: 45 A, -45 A ends each A in two A's, turned 45° left and right. *0.9 after a child shrinks it. A symbol with no rule is a leaf. Seeds start as the first rule's symbol. 45, -45 alone is short for the first example.

Angles and scales can move with the time t, in seconds: A: (45 + 20*sin(t)) A, -45 A. There's sin cos tri pulse abs min max clamp mix sqrt and pi.
