Plotting on Ordinals

Where on a line do you put 1, 2, 3, …, ω, ω+1, …, ωω, … so that you can plot a function of ordinals? Turn each ordinal into a tree, the tree into parentheses, the parentheses into binary digits, and the digits into a number. Pick an ordinal and follow it step by step, then zoom the ruler it makes. Plot a function

Drag the ruler to pan; scroll, pinch or + − to zoom; click a tick to pick it. ← → step to the ordinal before or after, N switches the parentheses, 0 fits. Type ordinals with w or ω, + * ^ and brackets: w^(w+1)*2+3, or 2^w^w. The ruler has every ordinal of up to 12 nodes, so ticks get finer as you zoom. Ordinal arithmetic and the parentheses are ported from the post's ordinals-python ↗, whose plots weight the digits by the golden ratio (r = 0.618).
