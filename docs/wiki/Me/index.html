Me 👋

I like meta-programming 🪞, applied logic 🧠, and simulation 🌍: languages that can reason about their own code, proofs that run ✅, and systems with a lot of moving parts ⚙️. On the side I enjoy puzzles 🧩, games 🎲, and the occasional trip past infinity ♾️ with ordinals, which is most of what ends up on this blog ✍️.

I was a Founding Engineer 🌱 at Sedaro 🛰️ and am now an Engineering Manager 🧑‍💼. I lead development of our compiler 🛠️ and runtime 🏃 for federated simulation and modeling. For more on my background, see my LinkedIn 👔.

Away from the keyboard 🌲, I enjoy mountain biking 🚵, skiing ⛷️, volleyball 🏐, and weight-lifting 🏋️.
